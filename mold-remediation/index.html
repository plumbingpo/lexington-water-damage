Treat mold as a warning as well as a health hazard, because the hidden moisture feeding it puts your property's structural integrity at risk. Mold exposure can cause respiratory issues, allergic reactions, headaches and more severe health effects, particularly in children, the elderly and people with compromised immune systems. At Lexington Restoration, our IICRC-certified mold remediation technicians follow strict industry protocols to safely contain, remove and prevent mold in Lexington homes and commercial properties.

Effective mold remediation goes far beyond scrubbing visible mold off surfaces. Done professionally, the work includes finding where the moisture comes from, containing the area so spores cannot cross-contaminate other rooms, taking out affected material under HEPA filtration, applying antimicrobials to what remains and correcting the moisture issue to prevent a return. Our comprehensive approach means mold is eliminated at the source, not just hidden for a while.

First comes a full assessment of the property, frequently with air quality tests that identify the kinds of spores in the air and how concentrated they are. Those findings shape a plan written for your situation instead of a generic one. IICRC S520 standards guide the whole job, which is done inside negative-pressure containment with HEPA air scrubbers running. Post-remediation verification, including clearance air testing when requested, confirms that your property meets safe occupancy standards.

Maintenance Tips for Mold Remediation

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly mold issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Make sure bathrooms, kitchens and laundry areas have adequate exhaust ventilation
  • Aim for 30 to 50% indoor humidity and never let it stay above 60%, running dehumidifiers and ventilating as needed
  • Address condensation on windows, pipes and cold surfaces promptly
  • Hang or launder wet fabrics promptly and wipe up spills as they happen
  • Repair any leak inside 24 hours, since 24 to 48 hours of moisture is all mold requires to begin
  • In bathrooms, basements and other damp areas, choose paint and drywall rated mold-resistant

Why Professional Mold Remediation Matters

Removing mold is work homeowners should not attempt on their own. Disturbing mold colonies without proper containment sends millions of spores airborne, which can contaminate unaffected areas of your home and create worse health exposure than the original problem. Professional mold remediation uses negative air pressure containment, HEPA-filtered air scrubbers and personal protective equipment so spores are captured and contained during removal.

A professional job goes after the moisture source too, because that is why the mold grew in the first place. Removing visible mold without correcting the underlying moisture problem means the mold will return, often within weeks. Our certified technicians are trained and equipped to track down moisture you cannot see, dry the affected areas the right way and make the corrections that keep mold from recurring.

Signs It Is Time for Mold Remediation

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Stains spreading on a ceiling or wall, or dark marks ringing the air vents
  • Cleaning that never lasts, with growth returning to the same surface
  • Visible mold growth of any color on walls, ceilings, flooring or other surfaces
  • Discovering a plumbing or roof leak that has been dripping for a long time
  • Warping, blistered paint or wallpaper lifting off the wall, all clues to moisture you cannot see
  • A flood or leak in the recent past that never got a professional dry-out
  • Windows, cold surfaces or walls that sweat with condensation on a regular basis
